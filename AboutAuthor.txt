At March 2026 I was building AI orchestration system, which orchestrates different AI system, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and literaly any other that come to work together on the same project.
My orchestration system aims to be consistently more clever, more capable and more productive, than any of these AIs can do alone.
The central piece of the orchestration system is a shared storage to which all non-temporary non-garbage files of a project under development, and in general all relevant files that worth to be accounted by an AI, 
to be automatically mirrored to a shared storage upon their creation, closure with debounce after an update, renaming, moving, metadata update or deletion. 
This is how a standalone SelectiveMirror windows service was born. I am pleased to make it an open source public utility.
